# Momentreel — Marketing Plan (pre-launch → launch)

**Context assumed:** solo founder, pre-launch (waitlist live), near-zero budget, paid-only product (7-day trial, card required). The strategy that fits: build in public + dogfood the product + borrow audiences. Adjust if any assumption is wrong.

## The one unfair advantage

**Momentreel can market itself.** Every clip the product generates is a demo. Your content engine is: run real podcasts/streams through the pipeline → post the best clips → caption them "made with Momentreel in 5 min". No other marketing channel is this cheap or this convincing.

---

## Top 5 plays (in priority order)

### 1. Dogfood content engine (short-form video, idea #43)
**Why it fits:** zero budget, infinite content supply, and the output *is* the product proof.
**How to start:**
1. Pick 5 popular podcasts with permissive clipping cultures (or CC-licensed / your own content) and generate clips.
2. Post 1 clip/day to a Momentreel TikTok + Shorts + Reels account, always with a before/after frame ("2h stream → this clip, automatically").
3. Track which clips drive profile visits → waitlist clicks (UTM links in bio).
**Success looks like:** one clip breaking 50k views; steady 10–30 waitlist signups/week from bio links.
**Needs:** ~30 min/day.

### 2. Build in public on X (idea #41)
**Why it fits:** solo founders get disproportionate reach on X; your buyers (creators) live there; it compounds into launch-day distribution.
**How to start:** post the launch thread (see `launch-posts.md`), then 3–4 posts/week from the templates: shipped features, real numbers, pipeline internals (the smart-crop story is genuinely interesting engineering content).
**Success looks like:** 1–2k followers by launch; your Product Hunt launch day is then warm, not cold.
**Needs:** 30 min/day, consistency over cleverness.

### 3. Reddit comment marketing (ideas #38 + #44)
**Why it fits:** people literally ask "how do I clip my podcast" and "OpusClip alternatives?" every week. High intent, free.
**How to start:** weekly saved searches for those phrases; answer thoroughly, disclose, soft-mention. One value-post per month in r/podcasting-type subs. Full playbook + ready posts in `launch-posts.md`.
**Success looks like:** 5–10 signups/week, and SEO juice — Reddit threads rank on Google for "opus clip alternative".
**Needs:** 1–2 h/week. Patience; never spam.

### 4. Free clips outreach → testimonials (customer language, idea #139)
**Why it fits:** pre-launch you need proof, feedback, and content rights. Micro-podcasters need clips. Perfect trade.
**How to start:** DM 5 podcasters/week with the template in `launch-posts.md` — run their episode first, lead with their actual clips.
**Success looks like:** 10 happy early users, 3 quotable testimonials for the landing page, permission to use their clips as demos.
**Needs:** ~2 h/week including processing time.

### 5. Comparison pages (idea #11) — start now, pays off in 3–6 months
**Why it fits:** "Momentreel vs OpusClip", "OpusClip alternatives", "Klap vs OpusClip" are high-intent searches you can win as a small player. No-free-plan products especially need to catch buyers at decision moment.
**How to start:** one honest comparison page per competitor (OpusClip, Klap, Vizard) under `/vs/`, using your real differentiators (smart AI crop strategies, speed, price).
**Success looks like:** ranking top-5 for "[competitor] alternative" → trial signups with the highest conversion rate of any channel.
**Needs:** a few hours per page; use the copywriting skill when you're ready.

## Launch sequence (when the product is ready)

1. **T-2 weeks:** waitlist referral nudge — email the list: "skip the queue: refer 2 friends, get a free month" (idea #79).
2. **T-1 week:** line up 10–15 supporters for Product Hunt day (your X followers + free-clips podcasters).
3. **Launch day:** Product Hunt (assets ready in `launch-posts.md`) + X thread + r/SideProject post, all the same day.
4. **T+1 week:** "what I learned launching" post — the launch retrospective is its own content.

## Weekly rhythm (≈6 h/week total)

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Mon | Generate + schedule the week's clips (engine #1) |
| Tue/Thu | X posts (engine #2) |
| Wed | Reddit searches + comments (engine #3) |
| Fri | 5 outreach DMs (engine #4) |
| Ongoing | 1 comparison page/month (engine #5) |

## What to skip (for now)

- **Paid ads** — no budget, and creator CAC via ads is brutal vs. organic clips.
- **Long-form SEO blog** — slower payoff than comparison pages; revisit post-launch.
- **A brand subreddit / Discord** — communities need members; build the audience first.
